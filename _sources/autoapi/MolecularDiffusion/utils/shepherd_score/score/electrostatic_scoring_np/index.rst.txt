.. py:function:: esp_combo_score_np(centers_w_H_1: numpy.ndarray, centers_w_H_2: numpy.ndarray, centers_1: numpy.ndarray, centers_2: numpy.ndarray, points_1: numpy.ndarray, points_2: numpy.ndarray, partial_charges_1: numpy.ndarray, partial_charges_2: numpy.ndarray, point_charges_1: numpy.ndarray, point_charges_2: numpy.ndarray, radii_1: numpy.ndarray, radii_2: numpy.ndarray, alpha: float, lam: float = 0.001, probe_radius: float = 1.0, esp_weight: float = 0.5) -> numpy.ndarray

   Computes a similarity score defined by ShaEP. It is a balanced score between electrostatics
   and shape similarity. Follows ShaEP formulation.

   :param centers_w_H_1: Coordinates of atom centers INCLUDING hydrogens of molecule 1.
                         Used for computing electrostatic potential.
                         Same for centers_w_H_2 except (M + m_H, 3).
   :type centers_w_H_1: np.ndarray (N + n_H, 3)
   :param centers_1: Coordinates of points for molecule 1 used to compute shape similarity.
                     Use atom centers for volumentric similarity. Use surface centers for surface similarity.
                     Same for centers except (M, 3) or (m_surf, 3).
   :type centers_1: np.ndarray (N, 3) or (n_surf, 3)
   :param points_1: Coordinates of surface points for molecule 1.
                    Same for points_2 except (m_surf, 3).
   :type points_1: np.ndarray (n_surf, 3)
   :param partial_charges_1: Partial charges corresponding to the atoms in centers_w_H_1.
                             Same for partial_charges_2 except (M + m_H,).
   :type partial_charges_1: np.ndarray (N + n_H,)
   :param point_charges_1: The electrostatic potential calculated at each surface point (points_1).
                           Same for point_charges_1 except (m_surf,)
   :type point_charges_1: np.ndarray (n_surf,)
   :param radii_1: vdW radii corresponding to the atoms in centers_w_H_1 (angstroms)
                   Same for radii_2 except (M + m_H,)
   :type radii_1: np.ndarray (N + n_H,)
   :param alpha: Gaussian width parameter used for shape similarity.
   :type alpha: float
   :param lam: Electrostatic potential weighting parameter (smaller = higher weight).
               0.001 was chosen as default based empirical observations of the distribution of scores
               generated by _esp_comparison before summation.
   :type lam: float (default = 0.001)
   :param probe_radius: Surface points found within vdW radii + probe radius will be masked out. Surface generation
                        uses a probe radius of 1.2 (radius of hydrogen) so we use a slightly lower radius for be
                        more tolerant.
   :type probe_radius: float (default = 1.0)
   :param esp_weight: Weight to be placed on electrostatic similarity with respect to shape similarity.
                      0 = only shape similarity
                      1 = only electrostatic similarity
   :type esp_weight: float (default = 0.5)

   :returns: Similarity score (range: [0, 1]). Higher is more similar.
   :rtype: np.ndarray (1,)


.. py:function:: get_overlap_esp_np(centers_1: numpy.ndarray, centers_2: numpy.ndarray, charges_1: numpy.ndarray, charges_2: numpy.ndarray, alpha: float = 0.81, lam: float = 0.3 * LAM_SCALING) -> numpy.ndarray

   Compute electrostatic similarity which weights Gaussian volume overlap by electrostatics.
   The Tanimoto score is used.

   Typically `lam=0.3*LAM_SCALING` is used for surface point clouds and `lam=0.1` for partial charge
   weighted volumetric overlap.

   :param centers_1: Coordinates for the sets of points representing molecule 1.
   :type centers_1: np.ndarray (N, 3)
   :param centers_2: Coordinates for the sets of points representing molecule 2.
   :type centers_2: np.ndarray (N, 3)
   :param charges_1: Electrostatic energy for the sets of points representing molecule 1.
   :type charges_1: np.ndarray (N,)
   :param charges_2: Electrostatic energy for the sets of points representing molecule 2.
   :type charges_2: np.ndarray (N,)
   :param alpha: Parameter controlling the width of the Gaussians.
   :type alpha: float
   :param lam: Parameter controlling the influence of electrostatics.
   :type lam: float

   :returns: Tanimoto similarities of electrostatics
   :rtype: np.ndarray (N,)
