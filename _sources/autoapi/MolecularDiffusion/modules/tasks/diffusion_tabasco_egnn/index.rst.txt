MolecularDiffusion.modules.tasks.diffusion_tabasco_egnn
=======================================================

.. py:module:: MolecularDiffusion.modules.tasks.diffusion_tabasco_egnn

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   TABASCO + EGNN backbone: a novel-model derivation of ``diffusion_tabasco``.

   Replaces TABASCO's ``TransformerModule`` backbone with ``EGNNBackbone``
   (``modules/models/tabasco_egnn/egnn_backbone.py``, wrapping this platform's
   existing ``EGNN``/``GCL``/``EquivariantUpdate``/``EquivariantBlock``
   layers). Every other TABASCO component -- ``FlowMatchingModel``,
   ``SDEMetricInterpolant``, ``DiscreteInterpolant``, the Euler-Maruyama
   sampler, ``TabascoNodeDistribution``, the pointcloud<->TensorDict adapters --
   is imported unmodified from
   ``MolecularDiffusion.modules.tasks.diffusion_tabasco``.

   See docs/model_novel/tabasco_egnn/INTEGRATION_PLAN.md ("Integration Plan",
   "Derivation Rung") for why this is a subclass that overrides only
   ``__init__`` rather than a smaller override: ``TabascoDiffusionTask.__init__``
   has no seam to override just the backbone-construction line, so this
   reproduces its assembly sequence (``diffusion_tabasco.py:280-326``) with one
   substitution -- ``EGNNBackbone(**egnn_config)`` in place of
   ``TransformerModule(**transformer_config)``.
