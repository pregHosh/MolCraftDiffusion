MolecularDiffusion.modules.models.geoldm.egnn
=============================================

.. py:module:: MolecularDiffusion.modules.models.geoldm.egnn

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   E(n)-equivariant graph conv layers, ported from GeoLDM's
   `egnn/egnn_new.py` (commit 03ae2031c712a1a6c1678e747bdcdc7a7560e00b).

   Self-contained per repo precedent (`modules/models/difflinker/egnn.py`,
   `modules/models/shepherd_arch`'s own EGNN) -- not shared with
   `modules/models/en_diffusion.py`'s own `EnVariationalDiffusion` machinery.
