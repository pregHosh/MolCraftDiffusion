.. py:function:: fourier_embedding(input: torch.Tensor)

   Fourier embedding for sigma in EDM.

   Assumes ``input`` is:
   sigma * w + b where w, b ~ N(0, I_dim)

   :param input: (B, dim) where B is the number of molecules in the batch and dim is the embedding dimension

   :returns: fourier embedding of shape (B, dim)


.. py:function:: positional_encoding(position, dim, device)

   Positional encoding for uniform timesteps.

   :param position: (B,) where B is the number of molecules in the batch
   :param dim: int, the dimension of the position embeddings
   :param device: device to store the position embeddings on

   :returns: position embeddings of shape (B, dim)
